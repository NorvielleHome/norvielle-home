Common questions

What blanket material is warmest for winter? +

Wool and chunky-knit acrylic blends trap the most heat, followed by sherpa fleece. Cotton waffle-knit throws are warm but more breathable — better for layering than for deep cold.

How big should a throw blanket be for a couch? +

A standard throw runs 50x60 inches, which drapes nicely over one or two people on a sofa. For a bed or a larger sectional, look for 60x80 inches or a true blanket size.

How do I stop a chunky-knit blanket from shedding? +

Hand wash or use a gentle cycle in a mesh bag, then air dry flat. Avoid the tumble dryer — it's the main cause of pilling and loose fibers on chunky knits.

Are weighted blankets good for cold weather? +

Weighted blankets add pressure, not necessarily warmth. Pick one with a warm outer fabric like flannel or sherpa if you want the calming weight and the cozy feel together.
